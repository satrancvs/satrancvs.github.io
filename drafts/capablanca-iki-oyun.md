The repetition of moves in the endgame plays an important role. Disregarding the fact that it gains time for thinking, it can be mentioned that, by repeating moves, the active side acquires certain psychological gains. In addition, repeating moves enables the position to be clarified to the maximum extent. We know that many upholders of 'pure' chess will severely criticize us for this advice.
